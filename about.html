About Me

I am a Bilingual Mechanical Engineering graduate, Summa Cum Laude, with nearly 1.5 years of co-op experience, starting my career at DART Aerospace as a Sustaining Engineering Specialist. My experience is primarily in testing and validation, mechanical design, product development, and engineering documentation. I enjoy working collaboratively in cross-functional teams, combining engineering design and analysis with practical implementation to develop reliable, manufacturable solutions.
